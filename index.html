Where Mathematics Meets Illumination

At Curve Lab, we start with code, mathematical curves, and computational geometry. By manipulating geometric rules, we sculpt fluid silhouettes and micro-textures that bend and soften light in ways traditional manufacturing cannot achieve.

Every piece is printed and assembled layer-by-layer in small batches, ensuring high precision with zero unnecessary material waste.
