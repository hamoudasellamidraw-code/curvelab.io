The idea

Where computation becomes something you can touch.

Curve Lab treats the algorithm as a design tool, not a visual effect. Curves are generated, edited and refined until mathematics becomes a physical object with presence, texture and atmosphere.

03 / Studio

A small design studio for curious spaces.

Curve Lab creates sculptural objects at the intersection of industrial design, computational geometry and digital fabrication.

We are interested in pieces that feel quiet at first, then reveal their construction as you move around them.
